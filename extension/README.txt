ChatBridge Capture - browser extension
=======================================

What it does: adds a "Save to ChatBridge" button on ChatGPT, Claude.ai and Gemini. Click it and the conversation you're
looking at is saved into the ChatBridge app on this PC. Nothing is read or sent until you click the button, and chats go
only to your own PC (127.0.0.1), never to a server.

Install (Edge or Chrome):
 1. Open edge://extensions (or chrome://extensions).
 2. Turn on "Developer mode".
 3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose this folder.
 4. Open ChatBridge -> Settings -> Browser capture -> "Copy code".
 5. Click the extension's icon -> it opens its options page. Paste the code and press Save, then "Test connection".

Use: open a chat on one of the sites, click the teal "Save to ChatBridge" button at the bottom-right (or the toolbar
icon). The chat appears in ChatBridge, grouped under where it came from.

If the button says it can't find the conversation, the site changed its page layout. Select the chat text on the page and
click again, and tell the developer which site it was.
