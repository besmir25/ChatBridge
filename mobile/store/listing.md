## English (default language, en-US)
**App name** (≤30): `ChatBridge: Move AI Chats`

**Short description** (≤80): `Move any AI chat, or just part of it, to another AI app. Private, no account.`

**Full description:**
Started a conversation in one AI and want to continue it in another? ChatBridge carries it over without starting from scratch.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Share a conversation to ChatBridge (or copy it, or add an exported file).
2. See it as sections and tick just the parts you want, or send it all. A short outline of the rest is added so the new AI sees the bigger picture.
3. Choose where it goes: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, Grok, DeepSeek or Le Chat (the ones installed on your phone), a website, a file, the clipboard, or any other app.

MADE FOR PRIVACY
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no servers.
• The app does not even ask for internet access. Your conversations stay on your phone.
• Passwords, keys, email addresses and phone numbers are hidden by default before anything is sent. It is a safeguard, not a guarantee, so look over what you send.
• ChatBridge never sends a message for you. It puts the text where you chose, and you press send.

ALSO
• Reads your exported ChatGPT and Claude data (the .zip file, as it is).
• Searchable list of your conversations, with rename and remove.
• A light look by day and a dark look at night, switching by the time you choose.
• Available in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Chinese and Japanese.
• Free and open source (MIT): github.com/besmir25/ChatBridge

ChatBridge is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft or any other AI company. Product names are trademarks of their owners.
