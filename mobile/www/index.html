How it works

1. Share a conversation to ChatBridge. In any AI app, open the conversation, tap Share and pick ChatBridge. Or copy it and tap “Paste what I copied”.

2. Pick the parts you want. Leave everything unticked to send it all, or tick sections or single messages.

3. Send it on. “Share to an app” lets you pick any AI app. “Copy and open” copies it and opens the app or site, where you paste it into the message box.

ChatBridge never sends anything for you, and your conversations stay on this phone. There is no account and no server.

Apps limit how much text can be shared in one go. If a very long conversation doesn’t arrive, share it as a file (.txt or .md) instead.
