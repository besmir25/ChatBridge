Is my information safe?
Everything stays on this computer. ChatBridge has no account and no servers. The only thing it ever looks up online is whether a newer version exists, and you can turn that off in Settings.
I don’t see my ChatGPT, Claude.ai or Gemini chats.
Those live on the websites, not on your computer. Either install the ChatBridge browser extension (Settings → Browser capture) and click Save to ChatBridge on a chat, or export your data from the website and use Add from a file.
The app opened but nothing was pasted.
Click in the app’s message box and press Ctrl+V. The conversation is on your clipboard. For websites, you can also attach the file ChatBridge saved.
What does ChatBridge change in my conversation?
It removes passwords, keys, email addresses, phone numbers and your Windows user name. If a conversation is very long, it trims big blocks of code or output so the AI can handle it, and tells you when it does. You can change both in Settings.
