How ChatBridge works
Continue a conversation in a different AI, without starting over.
1
Pick your chats
The list on the left shows chats ChatBridge found on this computer. Click a chat to tick it. Use Open a chat file or Paste text for anything else.
2
Choose where they go
The tiles on the right are your AI apps and websites. Click one. Missing yours? Press Refresh, or Choose tools.
3
Press the big button
The app opens and your chat is pasted in. Read it, then press Enter yourself. ChatBridge never sends anything for you.
Is my information safe?
Everything stays on this computer. ChatBridge has no account and no servers. The only thing it ever looks up online is whether a newer version exists, and you can turn that off in Settings.
I don’t see my ChatGPT, Claude.ai or Gemini chats.
Those live on the websites, not on your computer. Either install the ChatBridge browser extension (Settings → Browser capture) and click Save to ChatBridge on a chat, or export your data from the website and use Open a chat file.
The app opened but nothing was pasted.
Click in the app’s message box and press Ctrl+V. The chat is on your clipboard. For websites, you can also use Save as file… and attach the file.
What do “Shorten long text” and “Hide private details” do?
The first trims very long pieces so the chat is easier for the AI to handle. The second removes passwords, keys, email addresses, phone numbers and your Windows user name before sending.
